What's real vs. stubbed on this backend

Every list above is a real Postgres/SQLite-backed resource, and the plan on Today is computed server-side. Drafting an email or proposal really creates an Outbox row. Approving it really tries to send — but Gmail, WhatsApp, and Odoo are wired to raise a clear error until you add real credentials (see the backend README). That failure shows up honestly in Outbox and History rather than being hidden.
